Privacy policy

Draft — pending legal review

Last updated: July 2026. This is a plain-language draft written before launch. It describes what this site actually does today — not a boilerplate worst case. Questions: hello@enterium.ai.

What we collect

Exactly one thing on this site collects personal data: the AI-Visibility Snapshot form. If you submit it, we receive:

  • Your company website
  • Your category, in your buyer's words
  • The two competitors you named
  • Your work email
  • An optional buyer question you chose to share

Why we collect it

To do the work you asked for: run the prompt set, produce your snapshot, and email it to you within 48 hours. After delivery you get one follow-up email. That is the whole funnel — no drip sequence, no newsletter you didn't ask for.

Analytics and tracking

This site ships with analytics disabled by default. Consent mode starts at denied: no analytics or advertising storage is used, and no analytics script loads, unless you explicitly grant consent. If you never consent, nothing is tracked.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • Share your submission with third parties, except the form-processing and email providers needed to deliver your snapshot.
  • Add you to marketing lists without a separate, explicit opt-in.

Retention and deletion

We keep your submission while your request is active and for a short period afterward in case you follow up. Email hello@enterium.ai at any time to access, correct, or delete your data and we will do it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be flagged on this page before they take effect.
