The loop

Collect → Rank → Rewrite → Verify → Publish → Measure.

01 Collect
We sweep thousands of sources and extract the full text of what matters in a niche.
02 Rank
Every piece is read for meaning and scored against the niche corpus. Only the strongest signal survives triage.
03 Rewrite
Each story is researched and written as an original article — not summarized, not spun — grounded in retrieved facts with provenance.
04 Verify
Deterministic gates check every claim against the sources: fabrication, relevance, duplication, brand safety. What can't be grounded doesn't publish. Fabrication rate: down 52% since we started measuring it.
05 Publish
Straight to the sites it belongs on — ours or yours — formatted, referenced, linked.
06 Measure
Search impressions, rankings, crawler behavior, and AI citations, tracked over time. The results feed back into what we research next.

Publishing is half the job. Measurement is what makes the second half honest.

The rules we work by

Boring on purpose.

Grounding, or it doesn't ship.

Every factual claim in every article is tied to a retrieved source in a verification ledger. You can audit the audit.

Kill criteria, in writing.

Before an engagement starts we agree what failure looks like and when we stop. If the numbers don't move, the engagement ends. We'd rather be fired by a document than kept by ambiguity.

Metered costs, hard switch.

Every LLM call is metered at token level with real provider pricing, against a budget with a hard cutoff. You'll never get a surprise invoice from us — the machine physically can't produce one.

Candor over polish.

Our own network is young: ~322 search impressions a week and climbing, domain rating zero, trend up 135% on our anchor site. We publish these numbers because a company that hides its baseline will hide yours.
